# Marlo's mod Compilation

A unit pack for **Supreme Commander: Forged Alliance** (FAF): around 65 new or reworked units (UEF, Aeon, Cybran,
Seraphim; land, air, naval, structures; T1 to T4), their projectiles, effects and sounds. Some factions are
unfinished. The units are made or edited from vanilla units.

Status: playable pack, maintained occasionally (mod version 160). Not balanced for ladder play. Licensed under MIT.
